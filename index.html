Privacy Policy

PLAYCRAFT
Effective date: 2 September 2026
Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PLAYCRAFT ("PLAYCRAFT", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile games we publish on Google Play (each, a "Game"). Please read it before installing or playing.

The short version: we never ask for your name, email address or any other contact details. Our Games have no accounts and no sign-in, and your progress is saved only on your device. The Games show ads through Google AdMob, which also brings in ads from Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize, and some Games use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how many people play and how the Games are used. These services collect device and usage data, described in Sections 2 and 4.

1. Games covered by this policy

This policy applies to every game published by PLAYCRAFT:

  • Jelly Blast: Block Puzzle
  • Gem Blast
  • Playbox

Any new Game we publish is covered by this policy unless its Google Play listing links to a different policy.

2. Information PLAYCRAFT collects

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details. Our Games have no user accounts, no registration, no login and no cloud saves, and we operate no servers that receive data from the Games.

Analytics. Some of our Games use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, to understand how the Games are played. It collects the following directly from your device:

  • Identifiers: a random app-instance ID created when the Game is first opened, and your Android Advertising ID where your device settings allow it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, country and screen size
  • Approximate location at country or city level, derived from your IP address (Google Analytics does not log or store the IP address itself)
  • App usage: when the Game was first opened, play sessions and how long they last, and in some Games events such as a game started or finished or an ad shown

We see this information only as reports in the Firebase and Google Analytics consoles, for example how many people played, for how long and how many came back. It is not linked to your name or contact details. Google's description of how Firebase handles data is here: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Beyond Google Analytics for Firebase and the advertising services named in Section 4, our Games include no other third-party software that collects data: no crash reporting, attribution, tracking or social media SDKs.

3. Information stored on your device

Our Games save a small amount of data locally on your device so the Game works as expected between sessions. This typically includes your scores, levels or progress, in-game coins and boosters, and sound and settings preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us. Uninstalling the Game removes it, and some Games also let you erase it from their settings.

4. Information collected by our advertising partners

Our Games are free and are funded by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which fills some ad requests from two other ad networks through AdMob mediation. These are the only advertising services in our Games, and not every Game uses all of them:

  • Google AdMob — Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC
  • Unity Ads — Unity Technologies, served through AdMob mediation
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle) — Liftoff Mobile, Inc., served through AdMob mediation

These partners collect information directly from your device, under their own privacy policies rather than ours. This may include:

  • Device ID and other identifiers, including your Android Advertising ID, a resettable identifier used for advertising, and other device or account identifiers used to deliver ads and detect fraud
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, region and screen size
  • IP address, and the approximate location derived from it, usually at country or city level
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were requested, shown, viewed or tapped
  • App activity, meaning limited in-app interactions relating to when and how ads are delivered
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the Game and the advertising SDK, such as app launch time and error or crash data generated by the SDK itself

We do not receive this information in any form that identifies you. What we see is aggregate reporting: impression counts, fill rates and revenue totals.

You can read what these partners do with the data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity Privacy Policy — https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff Privacy Policy — https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/

5. Why this data is processed

Advertising data is used to display ads inside the Game, to measure how those ads perform, to avoid showing you the same ad repeatedly, and to detect invalid or fraudulent activity. Advertising is what allows the Game to be free to download and play.

Analytics data is used to understand how many people play, how often and for how long, which parts of a Game work well or need improving, and how the ads in a Game perform.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent, which you give or refuse through the prompt described in Section 6. The legal basis for non-personalised advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention and analytics is our legitimate interest in funding, operating and improving the Games.

6. Your choices and your rights

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. On first launch you are shown a consent prompt delivered through Google's User Messaging Platform, where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising. Where the Game provides a privacy option in its settings, you can change that choice at any time.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, on your device under Settings > Privacy > Ads. The exact path varies by manufacturer and Android version.
  • Analytics. Analytics data is tied to a random app-instance ID rather than to you. Clearing the Game's storage or reinstalling it starts a new ID.
  • Access, correction and deletion. PLAYCRAFT holds no data that identifies you by name or contact details. If you would like analytics data about your device deleted, or have any other request, write to us at the address in Section 10 and we will help. Requests relating to data held by our advertising partners can also be made directly to Google, Unity or Liftoff through the contact routes in their policies linked above.
  • California residents. PLAYCRAFT does not sell personal information for money. Our advertising partners may use your device's Advertising ID to show personalised ads, which California law (the CCPA and CPRA) can treat as "sharing" personal information. You can opt out at any time by turning off ad personalisation or deleting your Advertising ID as described above. Our advertising partners describe their own practices in their policies.

7. Children's privacy

Our Games are not directed at children under 13, and PLAYCRAFT does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through one of our Games, contact us at the address in Section 10 and we will act on it promptly.

8. Data retention and security

PLAYCRAFT keeps no data that identifies you by name or contact details. Google Analytics for Firebase keeps event-level analytics data for the retention period set for our project, no longer than 14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain. Data collected by our advertising partners is kept according to the retention schedules set out in their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add or replace an advertising or analytics partner. The current version is always published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect. Continuing to play a Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
